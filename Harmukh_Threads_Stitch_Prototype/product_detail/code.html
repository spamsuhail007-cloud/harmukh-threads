The Weaver's Mark

Each Heritage Saffron Pashmina requires approximately 180 hours of meticulous hand-weaving. The weaver's "mark"—a slight variation in the pattern tension—is a testament to the human hands that created it. This is not a flaw, but a fingerprint of authenticity.

  • check_circle Authentic Kani weave technique practiced for over 4 centuries.
